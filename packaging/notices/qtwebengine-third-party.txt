Qt WebEngine: the third-party components inside it
====================================================

Qt WebEngine is built on Chromium, and Chromium carries the work of many
projects. The list below is the one The Qt Company publishes for Qt WebEngine
(https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtwebengine-licensing.html), where each entry links to
its full licence text. The same texts are in the Qt source archive named in the
notices above, under qtwebengine/src/3rdparty/chromium. None of the GPL-licensed
entries is linked into or distributed with the Qt WebEngine libraries; Qt uses
them only to reach Linux system resources.
